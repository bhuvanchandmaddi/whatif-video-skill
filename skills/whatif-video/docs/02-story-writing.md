# 02 · Story writing

## 1. Picking the idea (all must be YES)
- [ ] **One simple rule** you can say in one breath: "every X makes you Y".
- [ ] **Visible**: the effect can be shown in 3D (size, age, floating, bubbles, money, crowds…), not just told.
- [ ] **Countable**: there is a number for the counter (cm, years, metres, ₹, thoughts, likes…).
- [ ] **Relatable** for 13-30 year-olds in India: school, exams, Mom, phone, friends, crush, siblings.
- [ ] **Escalates**: small at breakfast, huge by evening.
- [ ] **Has a twist** that applies the rule to someone unexpected (grandpa, brother, mom, everyone).
- [ ] **Ends on a question** people want to answer in comments.

Good: lie → shorter · phone check → older · laugh → float · hear thoughts · every step costs ₹10 · compliments make people glow.
Avoid: abstract ("what if gravity was 10 % weaker"), gore, politics, religion, real brands or people.

When asked for several ideas, vary the counter type, the main sets and the twist.

## 2. The 12 beats (scene ids in brackets)

| # | Beat | Scene id | English words | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flash-forward claim | `tease1` | 6-10 | The most extreme visual + shocking fact. Ends with "…". |
| 2 | Rewind reveal | `tease2` | 3-7 | "…just by lying." Normal state, `flash` + `rewind` sfx. |
| 3 | Hook question | `hook` | 10-14 | "What if …?" Has the `title`. Exactly the idea sentence. |
| 4 | Normal start | e.g. `wake` | 8-14 | Establish the counter at its normal value. |
| 5-7 | Small consequences | 2-3 scenes | 10-16 each | Each one = situation + action + counter change on a word. |
| 8 | Escalation | `montage` | 14-20 | Rapid list of triggers, counter rolls. |
| 9 | Curiosity line | end of 8 or 9 | 8-12 | "And wait till you see who/how…" sets up the payoff. |
| 10 | Twist | `twist` | 10-20 | Rule hits everyone / society / someone unexpected. |
| 11 | Payoff | e.g. `brother` | 10-18 | Funny or wholesome resolution of the curiosity line. |
| 12 | Ending question | `outro` | 5-10 | Has the `title`. Invites comments. |
| – | CTA | `cta` | `{cta}` | Fixed line from config. `cta: true, noCaptions: true, min: 3.2`. |

## 3. Sentence rules
- Second person, present tense: "You wake up…", "Mom walks in…".
- 3-12 words per sentence. One idea per sentence.
- Put the **cue word** (the number or reaction word) at the **end** of a sentence so it lands with a pause: "You say yes. Snap. 169."
- Speak numbers as digits in English (`169`); the counter will match the word `169`.
- No words the TTS will mispronounce (slang spellings, emojis, "lol"). Write "L O L" if needed.
- English total: 120-150 words → 45-60 s.

## 4. Write it, then read it aloud
Before translating, read the English out loud. If any sentence needs a breath in the middle, split it.
